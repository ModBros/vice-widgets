# Vice

Bring the neon sunset of Leonida to your PC monitoring. Vice is a set of widgets inspired by the HUD of Grand Theft Auto VI: your hardware stats as health and armor bars, wanted stars, cash and stock prices.

**Disclaimer**: Vice is a fan-made widget pack. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Widgets

- **Sunset:** a background of the coast at sunset, dusk or night, with a striped neon sun over the sea, a city skyline and palm trees.
- **Minimap:** the radar of the HUD with a slowly turning city map, a GPS route and blips, and up to three bars below it, like health, armor and special ability. A bar flashes red above its alert threshold.
- **Wanted Level:** up to ten stars that light up as a value rises. A full wanted level flashes.
- **Cash:** any value as a dollar amount that counts to every new value, with the last change fading out below it.
- **Stock Ticker:** a card of the in-game stock market with the current value, the change in green or red and a sparkline.
- **Stat Bar:** a segmented skill bar of the stats screen with a label and a value.
- **Mission Banner:** a big "Mission Passed" title with a subtitle and value, which turns into a red "Wasted" above a threshold. Also works as a plain heading.
- **Phone:** a smartphone with a clock, the date and up to four apps that each run an action, e.g. open a website or lock your PC.

## Fonts

The titles and the cash use the Pricedown font if it is installed on the device that shows the dashboard, and Impact otherwise. Every widget with text lets you pick another font.
